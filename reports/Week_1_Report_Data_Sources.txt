4. JUSTIFICATION FOR OPEN-SOURCE / PUBLIC DATASET UTILIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Platform Compliance & Ethics:
   Instagram strictly enforces anti-bot measures (HTTP 401/429 rate limits,
   mandatory login challenges, and CAPTCHAs). Automated unauthenticated web scraping
   violates Instagram's Terms of Service. Under strict internship guidelines, no
   credentials, proxy rotation, CAPTCHA evasion, or security circumvention were
   permitted.

2. Authenticity & Verifiability:
   Both datasets consist entirely of authentic public Instagram influencer profiles
   and real historical metrics. No synthetic, mocked, or fabricated accounts were
   created.

3. Reproducibility:
   Because the data files are permanently hosted on public GitHub repositories, the
   entire data engineering and transformation pipeline is 100% reproducible by any
   evaluator or auditor.

4. Academic & Analytical Legitimacy:
   Using reputable open-data samples provided by industry-standard data providers
   enables thorough analytics, clustering, and automation insights without compromising
   compliance or data integrity.
