1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
The objective of Step 4 is to develop a transparent, mathematically rigorous, and
reproducible Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) ranking system to evaluate and
rank all 2,561 verified influencers. The model integrates audience scale, audience
receptivity, creator consistency, and content discovery into a single composite
Influence Score on a standardized 0–100 scale.

================================================================================
2. SCORING DIMENSIONS & CONFIGURABLE WEIGHT CONFIGURATION
================================================================================
IMPORTANT: The weights below represent configurable initial baseline assumptions
designed for balanced influencer evaluation, NOT universal industry standards.

Dimension                 | Weight   | Primary Input Feature        | Transformation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Engagement Performance    |  40.0%  | engagement_rate              | 99th-pct Winsorized Log Min-Max
Audience Reach            |  25.0%  | followers                    | Natural Log ln(x+1) Min-Max
Posting Consistency       |  20.0%  | posting_frequency_per_month  | Linear Scaling (Ceiling: 30/mo)
Content Quality Signals   |  15.0%  | theme_count + hashtag_count  | Dual-Component Ceiling Scaling

================================================================================
3. MATHEMATICAL NORMALIZATION FORMULAS
================================================================================
A. Audience Reach Score (Weight: 25%, Scale: 0–100):
   - Because follower distributions follow an extreme power law (1 to 982,005 followers),
     linear scaling would artificially compress 99% of creators into near-zero scores.
   - Formula:
       log_followers = ln(followers + 1)
       reach_score   = ((log_followers - ln(min_f + 1)) / (ln(max_f + 1) - ln(min_f + 1))) * 100
     where min_f = 1 (score: 0.00), max_f = 982,005 (score: 100.00).
   - Median reach score: 44.80 (corresponding to median 708 followers).

B. Posting Consistency Score (Weight: 20%, Scale: 0–100):
   - Evaluates creator activity cadence against an active platform benchmark of 30 posts/month (daily cadence).
   - Formula:
       consistency_score = min(posting_frequency_per_month / 30.0, 1.0) * 100
   - Median consistency score: 27.77 (8.33 posts/month); 30.0 posts/month scores 100.00.

C. Content Quality Signals Score (Weight: 15%, Scale: 0–100):
   - Combines two defensible discovery and categorization signals without compounding collinearity:
     1. Thematic breadth: theme_count / 5.0 * 100 (sub-weight: 50%)
     2. Hashtag capacity: hashtag_count / 30.0 * 100 (sub-weight: 50%)
   - Formula:
       content_score = 0.50 * (theme_count / 5.0 * 100) + 0.50 * (hashtag_count / 30.0 * 100)
   - Accounts with 0 hashtags/themes receive 0.00; accounts with 5 themes and 30 hashtags receive 100.00.

D. Engagement Performance Score (Weight: 40%, Scale: 0–100):
   - Engagement rate contains extreme micro-audience ratio anomalies (e.g. 1 follower with 44 likes = 4,400% ER).
   - To avoid compressing 99% of normal accounts into near-zero scores, values are winsorized at the
     99th percentile (cap = 56.62%), then log-transformed and min-max scaled:
       log_er           = ln(1 + min(engagement_rate, cap_99))
       engagement_score = (log_er / ln(1 + cap_99)) * 100
   - Zero engagement scores exactly 0.00; median engagement rate (5.21%) scores 45.05; cap scores 100.00.

E. Composite Influence Score Formula:
   influence_score = (0.40 * engagement_score) + (0.25 * reach_score) + (0.20 * consistency_score) + (0.15 * content_score)
   Guaranteed bounded strictly within [0.00, 100.00].

================================================================================
4. DOUBLE-COUNTING SAFEGUARDS & TARGET ISOLATION
================================================================================
1. Interaction Isolation: Direct like counts and comment counts were strictly excluded from the Reach
   and Consistency dimensions. They only influence the model through engagement_rate to prevent
   counting audience activity twice.
2. Content Collinearity Protection: Theme count and hashtag count derive from the same source field.
   Rather than allowing each to consume independent primary dimensions, they were combined into a
   single bounded 15% Content dimension with documented sub-weights.
3. Ethical Labeling Protection: Profiles with low scores or zero engagement are labeled strictly as
   'Low-Engagement' or 'Dormant'. No creator is labeled as 'fraudulent' or 'fake' based solely on
   algorithmic scoring anomalies.

================================================================================
5. DETERMINISTIC TIE-BREAKING HIERARCHY
================================================================================
When influencers share identical rounded Influence Scores, ties are resolved deterministically:
1. Higher Engagement Score (primary 40% signal)
2. Higher Reach Score (secondary 25% signal)
3. Higher Consistency Score (tertiary 20% signal)
4. Higher Content Score (quaternary 15% signal)
5. Higher absolute follower count
6. Lexicographical username ordering (deterministic fallback)
