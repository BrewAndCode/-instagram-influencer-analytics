2. HEURISTIC AUTOMATION RISK INDEX ARCHITECTURE & WEIGHTS
================================================================================
The Automation Risk Index synthesizes four defensible behavioral indicators:

Indicator Dimension            | Weight   | Operational Logic & Bounds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cadence Risk                   | 25.0%    | Rigid daily posting (>=28/mo scores 100, <=5 scores 0)
Engagement Disconnect Risk     | 30.0%    | Audience reach scaled against ER suppression (<2.0% ER)
Interaction Asymmetry Risk     | 25.0%    | 0 likes on high followers / hidden likes / 0 comments
Metadata Absence Risk          | 20.0%    | Zero hashtags & themes (100) vs partial (30) vs full (0)

Formula:
  Automation Risk Index = 0.25*Cadence + 0.30*Disconnect + 0.25*Asymmetry + 0.20*Metadata
