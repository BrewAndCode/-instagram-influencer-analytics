3. CLUSTER SELECTION JUSTIFICATION (Why k = 3 was chosen)
================================================================================
A. Evaluation of k = 2:
   - While k=2 yields the highest mathematical Silhouette Score (0.4056), it provides
     an oversimplified binary division (small nano accounts vs larger accounts).
     Crucially, Cluster 1 at k=2 exhibits severe internal strain: 172 out of 521
     profiles (33.0%) have negative silhouette scores with a mean silhouette of only 0.0698.
B. Evaluation of k = 3 (OPTIMAL SELECTION):
   - Peak Silhouette Score among multi-cluster solutions (0.3318).
   - Lowest Davies-Bouldin Index (1.2996), indicating superior geometric compactness and separation.
   - Highest Calinski-Harabasz Index (815.39).
   - Very low misclassification rate: only 104 out of 2,561 samples (4.1%) have negative silhouettes.
   - Yields 3 actionable, non-trivial operational segments that align with social media dynamics.
C. Evaluation of k >= 4:
   - Increasing k from 3 to 4 drops the Silhouette Score steeply by 28.2% (from 0.3318 down to 0.2382).
   - The Davies-Bouldin index degrades from 1.2996 up to 1.4072.
   - Negative silhouette samples double from 104 (4.1%) up to 218 (8.5%), indicating that k=4
     forces artificial divisions across what is naturally a single continuous distribution.
