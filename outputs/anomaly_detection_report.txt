A. Extremely High Engagement Rates (> 100%):
   - Identified: 9 profiles (0.35% of dataset).

   - Data Quality Distinction: These profiles are MATHEMATICALLY VALID according to the
     internship formula: ER = (avg_likes + avg_comments) / followers * 100.
     In social media mechanics, when content reaches Instagram's Explore page or Reels
     recommendation feed, non-followers interact in volume without following, legitimately
     pushing ER above 100%. They are classified as 'Statistically Extreme Ratios', NOT data errors.

B. Zero Likes Despite Large Followings (> 10,000 followers):
   - Identified: 62 profiles.
   - Analysis: Accounts with tens or hundreds of thousands of followers that record 0 likes
     exhibit a profound disconnect. They reflect either dormant legacy followings or
     platform UI like-hiding behavior.

C. Hidden Likes Phenomenon (0 Likes with Active Positive Comments):
   - Identified: 46 profiles.
   - Platform Insight: Instagram natively allows creators to hide like counts from public display.
     When like counts are hidden, scrapers record 0 likes while comment counts remain visible.
     Classified as 'Potential Hidden Metrics', not automated fraud.

D. Active Daily Posting with Completely Zero Interaction:
   - Identified: 44 profiles posting >= 20 posts/month with 0% ER.
